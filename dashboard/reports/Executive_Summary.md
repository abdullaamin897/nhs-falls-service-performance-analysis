# Executive Summary

## Background

The Head of Community Services requested an investigation into whether the Falls service had experienced a decline in operational performance between January and June 2025.

## Objective

To determine whether operational data supported the reported decline by analysing key service performance indicators and identifying areas requiring further investigation.

## Approach

The dataset was validated and cleaned before analysing monthly operational KPIs including referral activity, appointment activity, average waiting time, completion rate and DNA rate. Clinician workload was also assessed to investigate whether referral allocation contributed to service performance.

## Findings

The analysis identified:

- An increase in referral demand.
- An increase in average waiting time.
- A reduction in completion rate.
- An increase in DNA rate.
- Workload becoming increasingly concentrated on one clinician.

These findings suggest that service performance deteriorated during the reporting period.

## Limitations

The available dataset did not include:

- Referral complexity
- Staff absence
- Clinic capacity
- Patient preference

Further investigation is therefore recommended before implementing operational changes.

## Recommendations

- Review referral allocation.
- Assess staffing capacity using WTE.
- Investigate referral complexity.
- Continue monitoring KPIs over time.
